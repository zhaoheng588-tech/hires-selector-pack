FAQ

Does it log in anywhere?

No. Nothing to sign up. Payments are one-time through the extension; your license key is stored locally and verified offline with a cryptographic signature.

Which sites are supported?

Amazon, AliExpress, Shopee, Lazada, Temu and Etsy product pages. Nothing is installed on the sites; the extension only reads images on pages you open.

Do I keep a license after paying?

Yes — the checkout page shows an activation code. Pay, copy the code, paste it in Settings → Pro. Or just enter the email you paid with and the code is fetched for you.

Will it survive website redesigns?

The reading rules are data, shipped from our server and updated about every 6 hours when pages change — no extension update needed.
