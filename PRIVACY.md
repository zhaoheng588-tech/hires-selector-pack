# Privacy Policy — HiRes (Product Image Downloader · SKU Export)

_Last updated: 14 September 2026_

> **Short version.** HiRes runs entirely in your browser. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and it never sells or shares data. The images, listings and settings it works with stay on your own computer. The only information that ever leaves your device is the email address you choose to type in when you activate a Pro licence — and only then.

## 1. Who we are

HiRes is a browser extension published by an independent developer. For any privacy question, write to **support@facailo.xyz**.

## 2. What the extension does with page content

HiRes adds a download panel to product pages on **Amazon, AliExpress, Shopee, Lazada, Temu and Etsy**. When you ask it to, it reads the images, variant (SKU) data, video links and product information already displayed on the page you have open, and saves the files you selected to your computer's Downloads folder.

- This reading happens **locally, inside your browser**. Page content is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
- HiRes runs only on the product pages listed above. It does not run on other websites.
- Collected file names, history entries and settings are stored in your browser's local extension storage on your machine.

## 3. The only data we receive

| What | When | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your email address | Only if you type it into the "Already purchased?" box to fetch your activation code | To find the payment record for that address and return the matching licence key |
| Your email address (as provided at checkout) | When you buy Pro through our checkout, the payment provider sends us the order notification | To issue the licence and to be able to help you later |

We do not receive, and do not want, any information about the products you look at, the pages you visit, the files you download or the searches you run. The extension has no telemetry.

## 4. What we never do

- We never sell, rent or share your data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.
- We never use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
- We never use your data for anything unrelated to the single purpose of the extension (collecting product media and listing data from the page you are viewing).
- We do not collect browsing history, keystrokes, health, financial or location data.

## 5. Reading rules

So that the extension keeps working when a shop changes its page layout, it downloads a small JSON **data** file (CSS selector strings, field names and numeric thresholds) from our server about once every 6 hours. That request contains no page content, no URL of yours and no identifier. It is data only — never code — and you can turn it off, or point it at your own address, in the extension's settings.

## 6. Payments

Pro is a one-time purchase. Payments are processed by our payment provider (Waffo / Pancake); card and wallet details are entered on their page and are never seen by the extension or by us. We receive only the order reference, the email address you supplied and the payment status.

## 7. Licence verification

Your licence key is a signed token that the extension verifies **offline** with a public key built into the package. It is stored locally on your device. Nothing about your usage is sent when it is checked.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Local data (history, settings, licence) lives on your machine and is removed when you clear the extension's data or uninstall it. Order records held by our payment provider and by us are kept as long as needed for accounting and support. To ask us to delete the email address associated with your order, write to **support@facailo.xyz**.

## 9. Children

HiRes is a tool for professional sellers and is not directed at children under 13.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, mention it in the extension's release notes.
